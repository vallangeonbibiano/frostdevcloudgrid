Why Choose Us

Small studio. Deep expertise. Quiet conviction.

frostdevcloudgrid is a deliberately small team. We don't pretend to be a 200-person agency, and we won't win every pitch. What we do is build software that respects its user — and we do it better than almost anyone.

PRINCIPLE 00

"Data never leaves your device."

This is not a marketing slogan. It is the architectural rule we apply to every product we ship. We do not write backend services for user data. We do not build analytics pipelines that touch user content. We do not accept SDKs that phone home. When you use software made by frostdevcloudgrid, your data stays on your hardware — encrypted, under your control, and visible to no one but you. This is not a competitive advantage we leverage in pitches. It is a non-negotiable constraint that shapes every technical decision we make.

PRINCIPLE 01

Senior-Only Engineering Team

Every line of code that ships from frostdevcloudgrid is written by a senior engineer with at least eight years of professional experience. We do not have junior staff. We do not have offshore teams. We do not have a "code review by AI" pass. The work is done by humans who have done it before, and it shows in every product we deliver.

8+ Years minimum per engineer
PRINCIPLE 02

Production Apps as Evidence

We could talk about our expertise all day. We choose to show it instead. Six production apps are currently live on the App Store, each one the result of a years-long investment in craft, performance, and user experience. They are the most honest portfolio we can offer — apps that real people use every day.

6 Live apps in App Store
PRINCIPLE 03

Zero Cloud Dependency

Our products are designed to work fully offline. No accounts, no subscriptions tied to remote services, no critical feature gated behind a server. If our infrastructure went dark tomorrow, every product we have ever shipped would continue to function identically for every user who already has it installed.

100% Functionality available offline
PRINCIPLE 04

Regulatory Compliance as Default

GDPR, CCPA, COPPA, LGPD, PIPEDA, UK PECR, China PIPL, and Australian Privacy Principles — we treat compliance as a baseline, not a feature. Every product ships with full data-minimization, clear consent flows, and an App Privacy Label that we have actually read and verified.

8+ Regulatory frameworks aligned
PRINCIPLE 05

End-to-End Ownership

From the first product discovery workshop to the last post-launch design audit, every project at frostdevcloudgrid has a single point of contact and a single team responsible for it. No hand-offs between "strategy", "design", and "engineering" departments. One team, one vision, one product.

1 Team from concept to launch
PRINCIPLE 06

Research-Driven Approach

About a third of studio time is dedicated to R&D — building tools that never ship, prototyping ideas that may never see a product, and contributing to open-source projects. This is how we stay sharp. This is how we deliver work that other studios can't.

33% Time spent in pure research
PRINCIPLE 07

Calm, Considered Communication

We do not chase hype. We do not use the words "AI-powered" unless the product genuinely is. We do not over-promise. We communicate the way we design — clearly, calmly, and with respect for your time.

48h Average response time to enquiries
Our Capabilities

The full stack, end to end.

From idea to App Store, from the first SwiftUI render to the last accessibility audit, our team has done it. Here is a representative cross-section of the capabilities we bring to every project.

Swift / SwiftUI / Combine

Native iOS, iPadOS, and macOS development with the modern Apple stack. Structured concurrency, async/await, and Combine where appropriate.

Privacy Engineering

Zero-knowledge architectures, encrypted local stores, App Privacy Labels, ATT prompt optimization, and threat modeling.

Audio Synthesis (AVF / AudioKit)

Generative audio engines, real-time DSP, procedural sample libraries, and sub-10ms latency on the iOS audio thread.

Motion & Visual Design

Figma design systems, Lottie animations, Metal-rendered micro-interactions, and 120Hz motion polish.

On-Device Machine Learning

CoreML, CreateML, and TensorFlow Lite. Quantized models under 8 MB. Zero inference calls leaving the device.

CI/CD & DevOps

Xcode Cloud, GitHub Actions, Fastlane, automated UI tests, signed TestFlight distributions, and weekly production builds.

Encrypted Local Storage

CoreData + CryptoKit, SQLite + SQLCipher, file-level AES-GCM-256, and the Keychain for credentials.

Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, color-contrast audits, and switch-control testing on real hardware.

Generative Systems

L-systems, cellular automata, reaction-diffusion, and procedural content engines built in-house.

Product Strategy

Two-week discovery engagements, written product theses, and technical roadmaps for early-stage teams.

App Store Submission

Metadata, screenshots, App Privacy Labels, and reviewer-relationship management. Rejection recovery.

QA & Test Automation

XCTest, UI testing, snapshot tests, and a 1000+ test case regression suite for every shipping app.
