Our Philosophy

Minimalist. Private. Uncompromisingly human.

Every line of code we write, every pixel we ship, and every byte we touch is filtered through three questions: Does it respect the user? Does it protect the user? Does it make the user feel calm, capable, and present?

We don't ship features for the sake of features. We don't add accounts, sign-ups, or cloud dependencies unless they are mathematically necessary. We don't accept tracking SDKs, ad networks, or analytics pipelines that compromise the integrity of the product or the user.

Minimalist Design

Every element earns its place. We remove until the product only contains what is necessary — and then refine what remains.

Privacy First

Local processing, zero tracking, encrypted storage. Your data is yours — we never see it, and we have no way to see it.

Experience First

Craft over speed. Detail over volume. We obsess over micro-interactions because the product is the sum of them.

Open & Honest

No dark patterns. No hidden clauses. We document what we do, why we do it, and what we explicitly choose not to do.

Engineering Stack

A technology stack chosen with intent.

We don't use tools because they are fashionable. We use them because they are the smallest, sharpest instruments for the job — and because they let us ship products that feel inevitable.

01 / CORE

Swift & SwiftUI

Native iOS, iPadOS, and macOS delivery. No cross-platform abstractions, no React Native — just the platform as Apple designed it.

02 / STORAGE

CoreData + CryptoKit

Encrypted local stores. AES-GCM-256 by default. Zero sync servers, zero plaintext at rest, zero telemetry leaving the device.

03 / AUDIO

AVFoundation + AudioKit

Generative audio engines, real-time DSP, procedural sample libraries. We build our own oscillators, envelopes, and effects chains.

04 / DESIGN

Figma + Lottie + Metal

Motion-first interface design. We render micro-animations directly on the GPU for buttery-smooth 120Hz transitions.

05 / PRIVACY

On-Device ML (CoreML)

Every "smart" feature runs locally. Recommendation engines, image classifiers, and natural-language tools all execute on the user's silicon.

06 / DELIVERY

Xcode Cloud + TestFlight

Continuous integration, automated UI tests, and a tightly-managed beta pipeline. We ship to production weekly, not monthly.

07 / COMPLIANCE

GDPR · CCPA · COPPA

Privacy by design from commit one. We don't bolt on compliance — we design with it as a first-class requirement.

08 / RESEARCH

Custom Generative Engines

Proprietary procedural systems for content, motion, and audio. We treat every release as a research artifact, not a release candidate.

How We Work

A four-stage studio process, refined across years.

Every engagement at frostdevcloudgrid follows the same calm, deliberate cadence. We work in small, senior-only teams, ship in short cycles, and write everything down.

01

Discovery

Two-week research sprint. Stakeholder interviews, technical audits, competitive landscape, and a written product thesis.

02

Prototype

Rapid, high-fidelity prototypes in Figma + SwiftUI. We validate the feeling of the product before we commit to a single line of production code.

03

Build

Two-week sprint cycles with daily demos. Continuous integration, automated UI testing, and weekly production builds on TestFlight.

04

Ship & Tend

App Store launch, post-launch instrumentation review, quarterly design audits, and a long-term maintenance contract to keep the product healthy.
